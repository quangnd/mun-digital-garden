A few months ago, I had an idea for a project management ecosystem with built-in AI. People call similar concepts things like "PM OS" or "PM Life", but I’ll call mine "PM Eco" for now.

The idea includes:

- **a prompt library**: for work and personal development (learn something)
- **skills**: that matter most for my work and personal life (risk analysis, planning)
- **artifacts**: small utilities that are useful in my daily work
- Pi-specific extensions
- **sub-agent personas**: BA, QA, architect, PM, and developer

Today, I can start with the Log Work artifact.

Actually, should it be an artifact, a skill, or a prompt? I might need a prompt to evaluate the options and make the decision.

The important point is that **this framework must make me stronger**, both physically and emotionally. It shouldn’t make me dependent on AI-generated thinking or tools. I also need a way to assess that mindset, something like what I’m exploring in another note about Doraemon's gadgets. This eye-opening experience is from a friend. I will talk about this one when I have a chance.

Enough talking. Time to build something...🤓
