title: Spaced repetition
tags:
  - learning
---

Spaced repetition is a way to remember things by reviewing them at growing intervals: a day, three days, a week, a month.

Why it works: each review happens just as the memory starts to fade, so the effort of recalling strengthens it.

A practical start: write one question per card, keep cards small, review a few minutes every day.
